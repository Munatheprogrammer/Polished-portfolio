About

Who I am

I'm Mirabel Munachimso Agu, a 300-level Computer Science student at Enugu State University of Science and Technology (ESUT), currently sharpening my frontend skills through the Genesys Tech Hub Upskill 26.1 program.

As part of Genesys, I contributed to building Vita Track — a hospital billing and collections platform designed to streamline staff records, billing clearance, and revenue management for healthcare institutions. Working on a real product with a team taught me how frontend development works in a professional setting.

I care about software that's accessible, functional, and genuinely useful — not just impressive in a demo. Outside of code, I'm motivated by something simple: showing that young African women belong at the front of the room in tech, not the back.
